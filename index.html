Science-Based Tracking

TenFit uses the Metabolic Equivalent of Task (MET) standard to ensure accuracy in every workout:

Kcal = (MET * 3.5 * Weight_kg) / 200

Privacy Policy

Data Collection: TenFit collects weight and height data exclusively to calculate calories using the MET formula. This data is stored securely in our Firebase Cloud Vault.

Authentication: We use Google and Apple Sign-In to provide a secure experience. Your email is only used for account identification and synchronization.

No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell or share your fitness data with third-party advertisers.
